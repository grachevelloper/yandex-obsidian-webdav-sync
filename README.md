# Yandex Disk WebDAV Sync

> Sync your Obsidian notes and attachments with Yandex Disk. Preview every change before applying it.

- **Two-way sync** exchanges changes between your devices and Yandex Disk.
- **Mirror sync** copies in one direction and shows planned deletions before you confirm.
- **File review** lets you compare versions, edit notes, resolve conflicts, and skip or delete individual files.

## Get started

1. Download the [latest release](https://github.com/grachevelloper/yandex-obsidian-webdav-sync/releases), place its plugin files in `<vault>/.obsidian/plugins/yandex-disk-sync/`, then restart Obsidian and enable the plugin.
2. Enter your Yandex account login, a Files WebDAV app password, and a dedicated Disk folder. Select **Test connection**.
3. Select **Preview sync**, choose a mode, review the changes, and confirm.

Your notes and attachments go directly to Yandex Disk. The app password is stored locally as plain text. Diagnostic logs stay on your device.
